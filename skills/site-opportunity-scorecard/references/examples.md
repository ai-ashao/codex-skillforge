# Worked Examples

These examples illustrate decision logic. They are not current market research and must not replace live evidence.

## Example 1: Markdown to image inside a broad Markdown converter

Assumptions:

- Host already offers ten Markdown conversion tools.
- Candidate focuses on social images, code-card output, templates, dimensions, and shareable exports.
- Candidate has a distinct image-first editor and content system.

Possible assessment:

- Independent keyword: 4
- SERP breakability: 3
- Long-tail expansion: 4
- User/use-case difference: 4
- Workflow difference: 4
- Brand reason: 3
- Link/distribution: 4
- Content system: 4
- Maintenance economics: 4
- Monetization fit: 3

Interpretation: a section or independent site may both be plausible. The deciding evidence is whether users search and link to the image-specific product independently and whether the host homepage would become incoherent.

## Example 2: Same ten tools, new domain and different homepage card order

Assumptions:

- Same users, same task, same pages, same outputs.
- Only the homepage hero and visual theme differ.

Expected result:

- differentiation: cosmetic;
- workflow difference: 0–1;
- brand reason: 0–1;
- content overlap risk: 5;
- intent overlap risk: 5;
- recommendation: existing-site page/section or reject duplicate site.

## Example 3: Game-specific compatibility tracker

Assumptions:

- Existing site is a general game tool suite.
- Candidate tracks game versions, mod compatibility, patch changes, community reports, and release history.
- Requires a maintained data model and update process.

Interpretation:

- Search and differentiation may be strong.
- Maintenance economics may be weak.
- A high opportunity score can still result in observe/reject if the operator requires low-maintenance sites.

## Anti-patterns

### False precision

Bad:

> Opportunity score 78.4 because the model estimates search volume at 18,000.

Good:

> Opportunity score 71/100, medium confidence. Search volume was not provided; SERP and query-breadth observations support the provisional score.

### Keyword permutation inflation

Bad long-tail evidence:

- converter online;
- free converter online;
- best free converter online;
- converter no login.

Better long-tail evidence:

- distinct input/output formats;
- audience-specific requirements;
- template libraries;
- troubleshooting guides;
- comparisons and standards;
- region/language pages with real localization.

### External-link fantasy

Bad:

> Bloggers may link to it.

Good:

> Technical writers can link to a public format-compatibility benchmark; template authors can link to a reusable export gallery; users can share generated assets with a canonical result URL.
